Terms

Terms of service

These terms cover appointments, orders and purchases with Lensmere Optical Co. (demo), in branch and through this website.

01Appointments

Online bookings are requests held for you until we confirm them. Please let us know as early as possible if you need to reschedule or cancel so we can offer the slot to someone else.

02Prices

Prices on this website are in PHP and include VAT where applicable. Prescription lens prices depend on your prescription and options; we'll quote before ordering. Prices and availability may change without notice.

03Orders and deposits

Made-to-order eyewear may require a deposit. The remaining balance is due when you collect your order. Estimated ready dates are guides; lens laboratories occasionally need more time and we'll keep you informed.

04Returns, refunds and warranty

Lenses made to your prescription are custom products. If something isn't right — the fit, the prescription or a defect — bring it back and we'll assess, adjust, remake or refund in line with our policy and your statutory rights. Frames carry a warranty against manufacturing defects; adjustments are free.

05Payments

We accept the payment methods listed in branch. Payments recorded at the counter are confirmed on your receipt; keep it for warranty claims.

06Contact

Questions about these terms? Contact us at hello@lensmere.example or call +63 2 8000 0100.
