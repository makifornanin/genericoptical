## Questions _(dynamic: websiteContent.faqs — first item open by default; the first 4 also appear on the home page)_

1. **Do I need an appointment for an eye exam?**
   Walk-ins are welcome when an optometrist is free, but booking guarantees your slot. Comprehensive exams take about 45 minutes.
2. **How long do prescription glasses take?**
   Most single-vision orders are ready in 3–5 days. Progressive and specialty lenses take 5–7 days because they're made to measure at our partner laboratory.
3. **Can I bring a prescription from another clinic?**
   Yes. Bring your written prescription (ideally less than a year old) and we'll fit lenses to it. We may recommend a quick check if it's older.
4. **Which payment methods do you accept?**
   Cash, credit and debit cards, bank transfer, GCash and Maya. Orders can be reserved with a deposit and settled at pickup.
5. **Do you fit contact lenses for first-time wearers?**
   Yes. A contact lens fitting includes measurements, trial lenses and a lesson on safe handling and care.
6. **Is there a warranty on frames?**
   Frames carry a 6-month warranty against manufacturing defects, and adjustments are always free.
