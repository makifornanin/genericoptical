About Lensmere Optical

It starts with listening.

Choosing eyewear is about more than picking a frame. We begin with a thorough examination, explain what we find in plain language, and help you weigh lens and frame options against how you actually live — screens, sport, driving, reading. From the first check to the final fitting, every visit is meant to feel considered and never rushed.

01What to expect

How we work

  • 01

    Thorough eye exams

    Refraction, eye-health screening and a clear explanation of your prescription.

  • 02

    Honest guidance

    Lens and frame recommendations that fit your needs and budget.

  • 03

    Made-to-measure lenses

    Single vision, progressives and specialty lenses from our partner laboratory.

  • 04

    Aftercare included

    Free adjustments and fit checks for the life of your frames.
