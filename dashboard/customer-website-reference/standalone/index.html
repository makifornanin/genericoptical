Common questions

Do I need an appointment for an eye exam? +

Walk-ins are welcome when an optometrist is free, but booking guarantees your slot. Comprehensive exams take about 45 minutes.

How long do prescription glasses take? +

Most single-vision orders are ready in 3–5 days. Progressive and specialty lenses take 5–7 days because they're made to measure at our partner laboratory.

Can I bring a prescription from another clinic? +

Yes. Bring your written prescription (ideally less than a year old) and we'll fit lenses to it. We may recommend a quick check if it's older.

Which payment methods do you accept? +

Cash, credit and debit cards, bank transfer, GCash and Maya. Orders can be reserved with a deposit and settled at pickup.
