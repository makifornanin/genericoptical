Lensmere Optical Co. (“we”) collects only the personal information needed to look after your eyes and your orders. This notice explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

01What we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, phone number, email address.
  • Appointment and order history, payments and balances.
  • Clinical information recorded during an examination, including prescriptions. This is health information and is handled with extra care.
  • Messages you send through this website, and the branch you prefer.

02Why we use it

  • To book, confirm and remind you about appointments.
  • To make, fit and deliver eyewear and contact lenses to your prescription.
  • To reply to enquiries and tell you when an order is ready.
  • To send offers or eye-care reminders — only if you opted in. You can opt out at any time.

03Online forms and bookings

The enquiry forms and booking calendar on this website are provided by our customer-communication platform, which stores what you submit on our behalf and sends confirmations and reminders. Details you submit are also copied into our practice-management system so our team can serve you in branch.

04Who can see it

Access is limited to staff who need it for their role; prescriptions are visible only to authorised clinical and dispensing staff. We share details with laboratories that make your lenses and with service providers that run our systems or send messages on our behalf, under confidentiality obligations. We do not sell personal information.

05How long we keep it

Clinical and financial records are kept for as long as the law requires. Enquiries that don't become appointments or orders are deleted or archived when no longer needed.

06Cookies

This website and the embedded forms and booking calendar use cookies that are needed for them to work securely. If we add analytics or advertising cookies, we will ask for your consent first where the law requires it.

07Your rights

Under the laws of your country you may ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw consent for marketing, by contacting privacy@lensmere.example. Some records must be retained for legal reasons; we'll explain if that applies.
