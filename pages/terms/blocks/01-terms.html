These terms cover appointments, orders and purchases with Lensmere Optical Co., in branch and through this website.

01Appointments

Online bookings are made through our booking calendar and confirmed by email or text. Please let us know as early as possible if you need to reschedule or cancel so we can offer the slot to someone else.

02Prices

Prices on this website are in PHP and include VAT where applicable. Prescription lens prices depend on your prescription and options; we'll quote before ordering. Prices and availability may change without notice, and stock differs by branch.

03Orders and deposits

Made-to-order eyewear may require a deposit. The remaining balance is due when you collect your order. Estimated ready dates are guides; lens laboratories occasionally need more time and we'll keep you informed.

04Online purchases

Where items can be bought online, payment is taken by our payment provider at checkout and you'll receive an order confirmation by email. Stock shown online is updated regularly but can occasionally sell out at the same moment in branch; if that happens we'll contact you to offer an alternative or a full refund.

05Returns, refunds and warranty

Lenses made to your prescription are custom products. If something isn't right — the fit, the prescription or a defect — bring it back and we'll assess, adjust, remake or refund in line with our policy and your statutory rights. Frames carry a warranty against manufacturing defects; adjustments are free.

06Payments

We accept the payment methods listed in branch. Payments made at the counter are confirmed on your receipt; keep it for warranty claims.
